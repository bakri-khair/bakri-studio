Privacy policy

Bakri Studio · Last updated 5 November 2026

Who uses this app

Bakri Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only after the channel owner signs in and grants access, the app uploads videos, captions and thumbnails, sets titles, descriptions and release times, and reads the channel's own videos, statistics and comments.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens and working files are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
